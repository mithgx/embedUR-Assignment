## **Subnetting 192.168.1.0/24 into 4 subnets**

### **The calculation**

To get 4 subnets, you need to borrow 2 bits from the host portion (2² \= 4).

Original mask: `/24` \= `255.255.255.0` New mask: `/26` \= `255.255.255.192` (added 2 bits)
