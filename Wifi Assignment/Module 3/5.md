# Wi-Fi Frequency Bands and Channels

Wi-Fi operates mainly in three frequency bands: 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, and 6 GHz. Each band is divided into multiple channels used for communication.

2.4 GHz Band

* Range: ~2.4–2.4835 GHz
* Channels: 1–14 (only 3 non-overlapping: 1, 6, 11)
* Characteristics: Long range, high interference due to congestion

5 GHz Band

* Range: ~5.15–5.825 GHz
* Many non-overlapping channels
* Supports wider channel widths (20/40/80/160 MHz)
* Lower interference, higher throughput

6 GHz Band (Wi-Fi 6E/7)

* Range: ~5.925–7.125 GHz
* Large number of clean channels
* No legacy device interference
* Supports very high throughput and low latency

Key concept:

* Wider channels → higher speed but more spectrum usage
* Narrow channels → better coexistence but lower throughput

Channel planning is critical to avoid co-channel and adjacent-channel interference in dense deployments.
