# PPDU, PLCP, and PMD in PHY Layer

In the 802.11 PHY layer, communication is structured using PPDU, PLCP, and PMD sublayers.

PPDU (PLCP Protocol Data Unit)
PPDU is the complete PHY-layer frame transmitted over the air. It consists of the preamble, PHY header, and payload (PSDU). It encapsulates the MAC frame for transmission.

PLCP (Physical Layer Convergence Procedure)
PLCP acts as an interface between the MAC layer and the PHY layer. It takes the MAC frame (MPDU), adds PHY-specific headers, and forms the PPDU. It ensures that higher layers are independent of the physical transmission details.

PMD (Physical Medium Dependent)
PMD is responsible for the actual transmission and reception of bits over the physical medium. It handles modulation, coding, signal generation, frequency selection, and radio transmission.

Summary:

* PPDU → transmitted PHY frame
* PLCP → formatting and adaptation layer
* PMD → actual radio transmission
