# Guard Interval in WLAN

Guard Interval (GI) is a small time gap inserted between OFDM symbols to prevent inter-symbol interference caused by multipath propagation.

In wireless environments, signals may arrive at slightly different times due to reflections. The guard interval ensures that delayed signals from the previous symbol do not interfere with the current symbol.

Types of Guard Intervals:

* Long GI: 800 ns (default, more robust)
* Short GI: 400 ns (reduced overhead)

Impact on efficiency:

* Short GI reduces symbol duration overhead, increasing data rate by ~10–11%
* However, it is more sensitive to delay spread and works best in environments with low multipath distortion

Thus, short GI improves efficiency and throughput but requires better signal conditions.
