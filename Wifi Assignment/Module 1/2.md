# Wi-Fi Devices and Their Capabilities

Common Wi-Fi devices used daily include smartphones, laptops, and smart TVs.

For example, a modern smartphone typically supports Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax), operates on both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands, and includes features like OFDMA and MU-MIMO. Similarly, modern laptops support high throughput and dual-band connectivity.

Wi-Fi generations are mapped as follows:

* Wi-Fi 4 → 802.11n
* Wi-Fi 5 → 802.11ac
* Wi-Fi 6 → 802.11ax

The classification depends on parameters such as modulation (e.g., 1024-QAM), channel width, and supported bands.
