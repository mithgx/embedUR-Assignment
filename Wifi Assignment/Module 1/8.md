# Difference between IEEE and WFA

IEEE (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers) is a standards development organization responsible for defining technical specifications such as the 802.11 Wi-Fi standards.

Wi-Fi Alliance (WFA) is an industry consortium that certifies devices for interoperability and compliance with IEEE standards. It also introduces branding terms like Wi-Fi 4, Wi-Fi 5, and Wi-Fi 6 for easier consumer understanding.

In summary, IEEE creates the standards, while WFA ensures devices correctly implement and interoperate using those standards.
