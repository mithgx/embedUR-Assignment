# 2.4 GHz vs 5 GHz Wi-Fi Observation

The following table summarizes typical observations when switching between 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz bands:

| Parameter           | 2.4 GHz                          | 5 GHz                            |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Frequency Band      | 2.4 GHz                          | 5 GHz                            |
| Range               | Higher (better wall penetration) | Lower (weaker through obstacles) |
| Speed               | Lower                            | Higher                           |
| Interference        | High (crowded band)              | Low (less congestion)            |
| Channels            | Limited (3 non-overlapping)      | Many available channels          |
| Latency             | Higher                           | Lower                            |
| Throughput observed | Moderate                         | High                             |

In practical testing, 2.4 GHz networks provide stable connectivity over longer distances, while 5 GHz networks deliver higher speeds when closer to the access point.
