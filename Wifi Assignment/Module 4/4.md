# Wi-Fi Scanning Process and Its Types

Scanning is the process by which a client discovers available Wi-Fi networks before connecting.

There are two main types:

1. Passive Scanning
   The client listens for **Beacon frames** periodically broadcast by Access Points (APs). These beacons contain information such as SSID, supported rates, security capabilities, and channel details.

   * No transmission from client
   * Lower power consumption
   * Slower discovery (depends on beacon interval)

2. Active Scanning
   The client actively sends **Probe Request** frames on different channels. APs respond with **Probe Response** frames containing network details.

   * Faster discovery
   * Higher power usage
   * Can discover hidden SSIDs (if directed probe is used)

Scanning Steps:

* Client selects channels to scan
* Performs passive listening or sends probe requests
* Collects AP information (RSSI, capabilities, security)
* Chooses best AP based on signal strength and policy

Scanning is critical for initial connection and roaming decisions.
