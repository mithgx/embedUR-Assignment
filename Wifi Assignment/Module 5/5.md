# Wi-Fi 6 vs Wi-Fi 6E Comparison

Wi-Fi 6 and Wi-Fi 6E share the same core technology (802.11ax) but differ in frequency bands and performance characteristics.

| Feature       | Wi-Fi 6                     | Wi-Fi 6E                           |
| ------------- | --------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Frequency     | 2.4 GHz + 5 GHz             | 2.4 GHz + 5 GHz + 6 GHz            |
| Range         | Better (especially 2.4 GHz) | Slightly lower (6 GHz attenuation) |
| Bandwidth     | Limited by crowded spectrum | Much higher (clean wide channels)  |
| Interference  | Moderate to high            | Very low (no legacy devices)       |
| Channel Count | Limited                     | Significantly higher               |
| Latency       | Moderate                    | Lower due to reduced congestion    |

Key takeaway:

* Wi-Fi 6 → efficiency improvement in existing spectrum
* Wi-Fi 6E → same efficiency + **massive new clean spectrum**

Thus, Wi-Fi 6E provides a real-world performance boost mainly due to reduced interference and wider usable bandwidth.
