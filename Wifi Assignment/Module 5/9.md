# Fast BSS Transition (802.11r)

802.11r enables fast roaming by reducing the delay caused by re-authentication when a client moves between APs.

Concept:

* Pre-establishes key material before roaming
* Avoids full authentication handshake during transition

How it works:

* Derives **PMK-R0 and PMK-R1 keys** from the original PMK
* Target AP already has required key material
* Client performs quick reassociation instead of full authentication

Benefits:

* **Significantly reduced roaming latency** (important for voice/video)
* Seamless handoff without noticeable interruption
* Critical for real-time applications (VoIP, video calls)

Without 802.11r, roaming delays can cause packet loss and session disruption.
