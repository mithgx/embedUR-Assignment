# Role of 802.11k and 802.11v in Roaming

802.11k and 802.11v are enhancements that assist clients in making better roaming decisions.

802.11k (Radio Resource Management):

* Provides **neighbor reports** (list of nearby APs with channel and signal info)
* Helps client avoid full channel scanning
* Reduces roaming delay

802.11v (Network Assisted Roaming):

* Allows AP to **guide clients** using BSS Transition Management requests
* Suggests better APs based on load and conditions
* Supports network optimization (load balancing)

Impact:

* Faster AP discovery (802.11k)
* Smarter roaming decisions (802.11v)
* Reduced latency and improved user experience

These mechanisms reduce unnecessary scanning and improve handoff efficiency.
