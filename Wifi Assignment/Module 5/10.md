# Combined Role of 802.11k, 802.11v, and 802.11r

802.11k, v, and r work together to provide seamless roaming in enterprise Wi-Fi networks.

End-to-end roaming process:

1. Discovery (802.11k)
   Client receives neighbor reports and identifies nearby APs quickly without scanning all channels.

2. Decision (802.11v)
   Network suggests optimal AP based on load, signal strength, and policy.

3. Transition (802.11r)
   Client performs fast handoff using pre-established keys, avoiding full authentication delay.

Combined benefits:

* Reduced scan time (k)
* Intelligent AP selection (v)
* Fast authentication (r)
* Minimal packet loss and latency

This combination enables **seamless mobility**, especially in enterprise environments like campuses, hospitals, and offices where uninterrupted connectivity is critical.
