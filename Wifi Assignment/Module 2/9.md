# Client Behavior when WLC Goes Down

When a wireless client is connected to a Lightweight AP operating in **local mode**, all client data traffic is tunneled to the WLC via CAPWAP.

If the WLC goes down:

* CAPWAP tunnels (control and data) are lost
* The AP cannot forward client traffic since it depends on the controller
* Existing client sessions are disrupted or dropped
* New client associations may fail

The AP may attempt to:

* Reconnect to the same or a backup WLC (if configured)
* Enter a fallback state (limited functionality)

In standard local mode, there is **no local switching**, so client connectivity is effectively lost until the controller becomes available again.

This is why modes like FlexConnect are preferred in environments where controller availability is uncertain.
