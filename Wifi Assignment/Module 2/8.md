# Challenges with Autonomous APs in Large Networks

Deploying more than 50 autonomous (standalone) APs in a large environment like a university introduces multiple operational challenges.

* No Centralized Management
  Each AP must be configured individually, making updates, policy changes, and troubleshooting time-consuming.

* Poor Scalability
  Managing large numbers of APs becomes inefficient and error-prone without a controller.

* Roaming Issues
  Lack of coordination between APs leads to slower or inconsistent roaming experiences for clients.

* Inconsistent Configuration
  Security settings, SSIDs, and RF parameters may vary across APs, causing instability.

* RF Management Limitations
  No centralized control for channel allocation or power adjustment, increasing interference and co-channel issues.

* Monitoring and Troubleshooting Difficulty
  No single dashboard to view network health, client stats, or performance metrics.

Because of these limitations, controller-based architectures are preferred for large-scale deployments.
